PAKOTEK APP PRIVACY POLICY

Effective from: 4 October 2026

1. WHO IS THE DATA CONTROLLER

The data controller within the meaning of the GDPR is: Marek Krawczuk, ul. Sosnowa 10, 07-300 Podborze.

Contact for privacy matters: mk@hrms.pl.

2. GENERAL PRINCIPLE: YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Pakotek is an app for creating packing lists for trips. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server of our own. Everything you enter (trips, places, dates, travellers, pets, lists of items, notes, templates) is stored only in your phone’s memory. We have no access to this data and we do not send it to ourselves or to any advertisers.

3. WHAT DATA THE APP PROCESSES

a) Data you enter: trip names, destinations, dates, names (or working names) of travellers, their age and optionally gender, names and weight of pets, list items and notes. It is stored locally until you delete it or uninstall the app. You enter data about third parties (e.g. children) on your own initiative - enter only as much as you need.

b) App settings (e.g. notifications, weather refresh) - stored locally.

c) Access data: the date of first launch (the start of the 7-day trial), the last observed clock time and a timestamp of the last subscription check. They are used to settle the trial and the subscription and to prevent the trial from being started multiple times (the controller’s legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES (THE ONLY INTERNET CONNECTIONS)

a) Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) - geocoding and weather. When you enter a destination, we send the place name you typed to the service (a request for coordinates). When you fetch a forecast, we send the place’s coordinates and the date range. We do not send names, lists or any identifiers. Like any server on the internet, Open-Meteo can see your device’s IP address. The service’s terms: open-meteo.com/en/terms. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing the function you requested). The feature is optional - you can create trips without weather.

b) Google Play (Billing) - purchase and verification of the subscription. Payments are handled by Google under its own terms; we do not see your card number or payment details. The app receives from Google information on whether the subscription is active. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

c) Google Block Store (Google Play services) - storing the trial start date so that it survives uninstalling the app (abuse prevention). The data is encrypted and may be copied to your Google account according to your device’s backup settings.

d) Android backup - if you have Google backup enabled, the system may copy the app’s database and access data to your Google account. This is a system feature that you control in your phone’s settings.

5. WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not show ads, we do not use analytics, tracking or an advertising ID, we do not sell or share data for marketing purposes and we do not profile you.

6. NOTIFICATIONS

Trip reminders are generated locally on your phone. You can withdraw your consent to notifications at any time in the system settings.

7. DATA EXPORT AND IMPORT

You can save your trips to a JSON file in a location of your choice and load it later. The file is yours alone - keep it secure.

8. RETENTION AND DELETION

App data is kept until you delete it (by deleting a trip in the app, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it). The trial start date in Block Store may remain on the device or in a Google backup after uninstalling - this is intentional; it can be removed by deleting the backup in your Google account settings or at your request after contacting us.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, uodo.gov.pl; in other countries: the data protection authority of your country of residence). Because your trip data is only on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights yourself in the app. For matters concerning access data, write to: mk@hrms.pl.

10. CHILDREN

The app is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The age and names of children that you enter in family lists stay locally on your phone.

11. SECURITY

Connections to external services use HTTPS encryption. Local data is protected by Android’s mechanisms (screen lock and encryption of the phone’s storage).

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change how we process data, we will update this document and its effective date, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.

13. IPHONE VERSION (APP STORE)

a) Apple (App Store, StoreKit) - purchase and verification of the subscription. Apple handles payments under its own rules; we never see your card number or payment details. The App only receives from Apple whether the subscription is active.

b) iOS Keychain - storing the start date of the trial period so that it survives uninstalling the app (abuse prevention). The data stays on the device and may be synced by Apple according to your iCloud settings.

c) Local notifications (trip and expiry reminders) are created on the device; we ask for permission when the first reminder is scheduled. We do not use remote notifications.

d) On iPhone we do not use device location, camera, photos or contacts; trip, backpack and profile data stays on the device, except for weather requests (Open-Meteo, HTTPS). Local data is protected by iOS data protection (screen lock and storage encryption).
